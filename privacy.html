Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Chrome browser extensions SnapFull and TableSnap ("the Extensions") published by LocalTools Studio.

1. Zero Data Collection

Both SnapFull and TableSnap are designed to operate 100% locally inside your browser. We do not collect, store, log, transmit, or sell your browsing history, webpage content, screenshots, extracted tables, or personal information.

2. Local-Only Permissions

The Extensions request only the minimum permissions necessary for their core single purpose: accessing the active tab strictly when you click the extension icon (activeTab, scripting), storing your local preferences and license status (storage), and saving exported files directly to your device (downloads).

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you choose to purchase a one-time Pro license, payments and global tax compliance are processed securely by our Merchant of Record (Creem or Paddle). When you enter a license key inside the Extension, only that license key is transmitted to verify activation status, and the activation result is stored locally via chrome.storage.local.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, tracking pixels, or data brokers in our Extensions.
